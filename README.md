## 🦉 What is NYCTAVIS?

NYCTAVIS is a desktop app for Windows that helps you understand and reclaim your disk space — safely. It scans your system, surfaces junk and true duplicates, and guides you through cleanup where **scan findings are not removed without your confirmation**. Disk analysis runs **on your computer**: no cloud upload, no telemetry, and no NYCTAVIS account required for Free.

> **Available now on the Microsoft Store** — free download with an optional one-time Pro upgrade.

## ✨ Features

- 🔍 **Deep disk scan** — quick, full or folder-picked, to map exactly what's using your space.
- 🧬 **Duplicate finder** — content-based hashing detects real duplicates, even with different names.
- 🧹 **Guided cleanup** — temporary files, caches, the Recycle Bin and Windows cleanup tools, with clear previews and confirmations.
- 💾 **Reclaim space** — review recoverable space and apply the actions that are available to you.
- 🛡️ **Safe by default** — explicit confirmation for potentially destructive actions and managed quarantine for reversible flows.
- 🔒 **Private & local** — no cloud upload, no telemetry and no NYCTAVIS account required for Free.
- 🌐 **Trilingual** — English, Español, Français.

## 🔒 Privacy first

NYCTAVIS is **local-first by design**:

- **No cloud** — analysis happens entirely on your machine.
- **No telemetry** — we don't track what you scan or delete.
- **No NYCTAVIS account for Free** — install and use the free edition without signing up with NYCTAVIS.
- **Temporary managed quarantine** — restore applicable items during the 20-day retention period.
