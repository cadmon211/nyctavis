Privacy first

Your files never leave your PC

NYCTAVIS is local-first by design. It reads your disk to help you clean it — and that is where everything stays.

  • ✔No cloud. Analysis happens entirely on your machine.
  • ✔No telemetry. We don't track what you scan or delete.
  • ✔No account. Install and use it — no sign-up required.
  • ✔Reversible. Quarantine lets you restore anything before it's gone.
Questions

Frequently asked

Is NYCTAVIS free?

NYCTAVIS will be available on the Microsoft Store with a free tier and an optional Pro upgrade. Pricing will be announced at launch.

Is my data safe?

Yes. NYCTAVIS runs entirely on your computer. It never uploads your files, never sends telemetry and does not require an account.

Will it delete files automatically?

No. Nothing is removed without your explicit confirmation, and cleaned items go to a reversible quarantine first.

Which Windows versions are supported?

NYCTAVIS targets 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11.
